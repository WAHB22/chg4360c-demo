# Credits

## Drawings

Every drawing on the page is original line art made for this app and drawn in code in `ui/motifs.js`:
the FINANCE wordmark, the amphora (tuition jars), the Ionic column (bills), the olive branch and wreath,
the owl coin, the waves, the Fates' thread, the four point star, the arch window, the pomegranate,
the Greek key meander, the torn paper edge and the engraved temple in the hero (a vertical line screen
generated from code, not taken from a photo).

No third party images are used. Nothing is traced or copied from the Minos element pack or any other
paid or licensed illustration set; the three reference images in the style guide were looked at for
mood only and are not part of the app.

If a photo is ever added (for example a Met Open Access object turned into the same line screen),
it gets its own line here with its link and licence (CC0 for Met Open Access).
